## Tab Wise 2.6

Version 2.6 is for moving through the panel without reaching for the mouse, and for seeing the groups you already made in Chrome.

### Keys for the open panel

`j` and `k` move, Enter opens, `x` closes, `s` sleeps, `r` reloads, and `n` starts a new tab. `/` focuses search. Arrows work the same way.

### A command palette

Press Command+K on Mac, or Ctrl+K elsewhere. From there: a new tab, search, this window or all windows, a new group, activity, sleep or close unused tabs, and close duplicate extras.

### Chrome’s own groups

Groups you made in Chrome show in the panel, with their name and color. A setting can hide them.

### Localhost stays together

Loopback tabs group as Localhost. Plain IP addresses stay as their own group instead of a broken domain name.
